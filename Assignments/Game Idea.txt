Game Idea

1. Sunny's Tamagotchi Reskinning
Genre: Visual Pet
Inspiration: 
	I want to fulfill my childhood desire of owning a Tamagotchi.
	However, I notice that the game requires a lot of attention without player's valuntary (The pet dies after being neglet for too long)
	Because of that, I would like to recreate Tamagotchi visual pet style game without high stake loss.
Game mechanics:
	Choose a pet
	Feed the pet
	Clean the litter
	Give a medicine
	Pet the pet
	Shower the pet
	Brush the pet hair
	Gain/ lost trust
Concerns:
	Timing (how to calculate the timing of pet's needs without notification system)
	Economy system (ex. trust/ happyness/ tireness/ health/ growth gadge)
	End goal of the game (might relate to theme ex. finding a new home for strayed dogs/ cat, befriending with a crow.)


2. Seagull Illegal ;P
Genre: Endless Runner, Space-shooting
Inspiration:
	Incase the Tamagotchi one is too overwhelming for the project and diminish my opportunities to learn, this one would be more flexible. 
	My first idea is about creating a game that encourage tourists to avoid holding food in open space and seagull attack. Additionally, the game can send the message about the seagulls are good for cleaning the beach (if player collect enough trash, player unlock a badge).
Goal of The Game: Sustain the hunger bar by collecting food and avoiding hitting obstacles
End of The Game: The hunger bar reduces to zero
Game Mechanics:
	Move up, left, right, and down
	Collect food (trash, ice-cream, burger, small shells)
	Avoid obstacles (Human, pole, trash can)
